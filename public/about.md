# About Meelte

> Meelte is a hyperlocal community platform built for Indian neighborhoods. It shows time-boxed offers from nearby shops, local events you can request to join, and one-to-one chat with the people and businesses you meet around you — without ever selling or sharing user location.

## Who We Are
Meelte is a community-driven, hyperlocal platform for India. It brings what is happening close to where you live into one place: time-boxed offers from independent shops, local events you can ask to join, and the people and businesses you follow. Meelte is free for community members and is available as an Android app on the Google Play Store. The web app is still in development and is not live yet.

## Our Mission
Meelte exists to make local life easier, more connected, and more rewarding. We replace overwhelming global feeds and expensive, untargeted advertising with a focused platform where neighbours genuinely connect, local businesses reach nearby customers directly, and opportunities close to home are never missed.

## The Problems We Solve
- **Information Overload**: Traditional social networks surface distant, irrelevant content instead of what is happening around you.
- **Fleeting Local Opportunities**: Local offers and community gatherings often disappear before neighbours even discover them.
- **Urban Isolation & Relocation Friction**: Moving to a new city makes it hard to meet people who share your language, culture, and interests.
- **Local Merchant Visibility**: Small neighborhood businesses cannot afford expensive, untargeted ad platforms and lose margin to delivery middlemen.

## What You Can Do on Meelte
- **Discover nearby offers**: Browse time-boxed ads from local shops and service businesses, with a price or discount and a fixed run of dates.
- **Join local events**: See events with a date, time, pinned location and joining cost, then request to join alone or with a group for the host to approve.
- **Host your own event**: Publish a public or private event, review join requests with group sizes, and remove attendees if needed.
- **Connect and chat**: Add friends, follow the shops and hosts you like, and talk one to one with text, documents, location sharing, and audio or video calls.
- **Grow your business**: Promote products and services to nearby residents and hear from them directly.

## Coverage & Availability
Meelte is live as an Android app, with operational hubs in **Bengaluru**, **Gaya**, and **Ranchi** where shops are signed up in person by our field team. The web app is in development and will be announced on this site once it is ready.

## Our Privacy Commitment
Privacy is foundational to Meelte. Your location is used to sort what is near you and to decide which nearby city zone applies. We never log, monetize, package, or sell user location coordinates or browsing history to third-party ad brokers.

## What Meelte Does Not Do
Meelte does not sell goods, run events, take payments or broker transactions. Purchases are settled directly with the shop, and any event cost is settled directly with the host. Meelte does not operate a jobs board, and it does not verify the identity of individual users.
