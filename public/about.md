## Who We Are
meelTe is a community-driven, hyperlocal platform for India. It brings what is happening close to where you live into one place: time-boxed offers from independent shops, local events you can ask to join, and the people and businesses you follow. The app is published on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone. The web app is still in development and is not live yet.

## Our Mission
meelTe aims to make local discovery and connection easier. The platform focuses on nearby offers, local events, and community connections rather than a global feed.

## The Problems We Solve
- **Information Overload**: Traditional social networks surface distant, irrelevant content instead of what is happening around you.
- **Fleeting Local Opportunities**: Local offers and community gatherings often disappear before neighbours even discover them.
- **Urban Isolation & Relocation Friction**: Moving to a new city makes it hard to meet people who share your language, culture, and interests.
- **Local Merchant Visibility**: Local businesses can publish time-boxed offers for nearby residents through the app.

## What You Can Do on meelTe
- **Discover nearby offers**: Browse time-boxed ads from local shops and service businesses, with a price or discount and a fixed run of dates.
- **Join local events**: See events with a date, time, pinned location and joining cost, then request to join alone or with a group for the host to approve.
- **Host your own event**: Publish a public or private event, review join requests with group sizes, and remove attendees if needed.
- **Connect and chat**: Add friends, follow the shops and hosts you like, and talk one to one with text, documents, location sharing, and audio or video calls.
- **Grow your business**: Publish time-boxed offers for nearby residents. People can interact with offers in the app.

## Coverage & Availability
meelTe is live as an app on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone, with in-person shop onboarding in **Bengaluru**, **Gaya**, and **Ranchi**. Nearby listings depend on configured city zones. The web app is in development and is not live.

## Our Privacy Commitment
meelTe uses location to sort nearby listings and determine which configured city zone applies. meelTe states that it does not sell or share user location with advertisers. See the [Privacy Policy](https://meelTe.com/privacy) for details about personal data.

## What meelTe Does Not Do
meelTe does not sell goods, run events, take payments or broker transactions. Purchases are settled directly with the shop, and any event cost is settled directly with the host. meelTe does not operate a jobs board, and it does not verify the identity of individual users.
