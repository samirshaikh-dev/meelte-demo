# About Meelte

> Meelte is a hyper-local community and commerce platform built for Indian neighborhoods. It connects residents with nearby food, fashion, events, services, health providers, neighborhood communities, and local job opportunities — without ever selling or sharing user location.

Official Website: https://meelte.com/about
Contact: support@meelte.com

---

## Who We Are
Meelte is a community-driven, hyper-local platform for India. It brings everything that matters within a few kilometres of where you live into one place: nearby cafes and boutiques, trusted local services, wellness providers, community events, and verified local jobs. Meelte is free for community members and is available as an Android app on the Google Play Store, with a companion web experience for any device.

## Our Mission
Meelte exists to make local life easier, more connected, and more rewarding. We replace overwhelming global feeds and expensive, untargeted advertising with a focused platform where neighbors genuinely connect, local businesses reach nearby customers with zero middleman commission, and opportunities close to home are never missed.

## The Problems We Solve
- **Information Overload**: Traditional social networks surface distant, irrelevant content instead of what is happening around you.
- **Fleeting Local Opportunities**: Local offers, gatherings, and community events often disappear before neighbors even discover them.
- **Urban Isolation & Relocation Friction**: Moving to a new city makes it hard to meet people who share your language, culture, and interests.
- **Local Merchant Visibility**: Small neighborhood businesses cannot afford expensive, untargeted ad platforms and lose margin to delivery middlemen.

## What You Can Do on Meelte
- **Discover**: Explore nearby food, fashion, events, services, and health providers across 5 curated categories.
- **Connect**: Find and meet neighbors who share your language, culture, hobbies, and goals.
- **Create**: Organize, publish, and host community events, interest clubs, and neighborhood gatherings.
- **Grow**: Promote your local business to nearby customers with 0% commission and direct inquiries.
- **Find Local Jobs**: Browse verified neighborhood openings and apply with a resume upload — or post openings as a local employer.

## Coverage & Availability
Meelte is available across India, with active community hubs in **Bengaluru**, **Gaya**, and **Ranchi** where localized community groups and verified physical storefronts are established. The platform is actively expanding to additional Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities across India.

## Our Privacy Commitment
Privacy is foundational to Meelte. Your location is strictly processed on-device to filter and display nearby listings and community meetups. We never log, monetize, package, or sell user location coordinates or browsing history to third-party ad brokers.
