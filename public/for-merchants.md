# List Your Shop on Meelte — Merchant Guide

> Meelte gives independent shops, restaurants, clinics, and service businesses a way to publish time-boxed offers for nearby residents. Shops are onboarded in person by Meelte's field team. Merchant pricing is not published.

## Who This Is For

- Independent shops and storefronts that want local visibility without running digital marketing.
- Restaurants, cafes, and food businesses that want to show a menu or today's offer.
- Service businesses — repairs, home services, professional assistance — that want nearby customers to find them.
- Clinics, fitness studios, and wellness centres promoting a time-boxed offer.
- Boutiques and apparel retailers that want to show a catalogue rather than a single item.

Meelte is a local discovery platform, not a delivery marketplace or payments provider.

---

## What a Merchant Can Publish

- **A single-product offer**, or a **shop catalogue of up to 100 products**.
- **Pricing** as an original and final price, or as a flat or "up to" percentage discount.
- **A fixed run of dates**: every ad runs from 12:00 AM on its start date to 11:59 PM on its end date.
- **A pinned location**, so residents get one-tap Google Maps directions to the shop.
- **Menu or price-list import from a photo**: photographing a menu fills in item names and prices automatically.
- **Generated product images** for items that were not photographed.
- **Follower updates**: residents can follow the shop and hear about its next offer.

---

## How Onboarding Works

1. **Submit an enquiry.** Send your business name, category, phone number, and city, or call the merchant desk directly on +91-8884524444.
2. **Meet the field team.** A member of Meelte's field team visits the shop in person and sets up the profile.
3. **Publish your first offer.** Add a single product or a catalogue of up to 100 items with a price or discount and the dates it should run. It goes live at 12:00 AM on the start date.

Onboarding is deliberately in person. Meelte's defensible advantage is a field team that signs up real storefronts, not a self-serve listing form.

## What Meelte Does Not Provide

Being explicit here prevents misquotation:

- **No payments or escrow.** Meelte does not process payments. Purchases are settled directly with the shop.
- **No delivery or logistics.** Meelte does not deliver goods or broker transactions.
- **No reach, views, or footfall analytics.** There is no merchant dashboard and no performance reporting.
- **No identity verification badge.** Shops are onboarded in person by the field team; that is not a public verification programme.

Meelte's field team currently onboards shops in **Bengaluru**, **Gaya**, and **Ranchi**. Nearby listing availability depends on configured city zones. See https://meelte.com/coverage.md for the coverage model.

## Who Sees a Merchant's Offer

Residents using the Meelte app, browsing by distance from their own location. The "Max distance" filter defaults to 60 km and can be narrowed, so an offer is shown to people near the shop rather than to a national audience. Viewers can like, comment, share, get directions, and follow the shop.
