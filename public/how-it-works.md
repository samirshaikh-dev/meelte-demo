# How Meelte Works — Step by Step

> Meelte is a hyperlocal community app for India. Residents browse time-boxed offers from nearby shops and local events sorted by distance, request to join events, and chat one to one with the people and businesses they meet. Event hosts and shopkeepers use the same app to reach the neighbourhood.

## Who Meelte Is For

Meelte serves four roles in the neighbourhood ecosystem:

1. **Everyday residents** — browse time-boxed offers from nearby shops and local events, sorted by distance, with directions to the shop or venue.
2. **Shops and service businesses** — publish a single offer or a catalogue of up to 100 products for nearby residents.
3. **Event hosts and clubs** — publish public or private events with a joining cost, and control the guest list through join requests.
4. **Neighbours and new arrivals** — follow shops and hosts and talk one to one with people they connect with in the app. The broader Discover People feature is still rolling out.

---

## Getting Started as a Resident

1. **Download the Meelte app.** Install it from the Google Play Store. The Android app is live; the web app is still in development.
2. **Set your maximum distance.** Allow location access so the feed can be sorted by distance. The "Max distance" filter defaults to 60 km and can be narrowed.
3. **Browse offers and events nearby.** See time-boxed offers from nearby shops and local events with their joining cost. Offers leave the feed when their end date passes.
4. **Take the next step.** Get one-tap Google Maps directions to a shop or venue, follow shops for updates, or ask a host to join an event. Meelte does not process purchases or event payments; any payment is settled directly with the shop or host.

---

## Getting Started as an Event Host

1. **Create the event** — category, joining cost in ₹, start and end time, cover image, pinned location, and public or private visibility.
2. **Share it** — public events are discoverable nearby; private events are shared with the people the host chooses.
3. **Review join requests** — attendees request to join alone or with a group, stating the group size.
4. **Approve, reject, or remove** — the host decides who attends and can remove attendees later.
5. **Collect the fee directly** — any joining cost is paid by the attendee to the host, not through Meelte.

---

## Getting Started as a Shop or Service Business

1. **Submit an enquiry** with the business name, category, phone number, and city.
2. **Meet the field team** — a Meelte field team member visits the shop in person and sets up the profile.
3. **Publish the first offer** — a single product or a catalogue of up to 100 items, with a price or discount and the dates it runs.

Full detail: https://meelte.com/for-merchants.md

## What Happens After You Find Something

- **An offer**: like it, comment, share it, open one-tap Google Maps directions to the shop, follow the shop for its next offer. The purchase is settled directly with the shop.
- **An event**: send a join request with your group size, wait for the host to approve, then attend. Any joining cost is paid directly to the host.
- **A person or a shop**: add as a friend or follow, then chat one to one — text, emoji, documents, and location sharing — or start an audio or video call. Everyone can block and report.

---

## What Meelte Does Not Do

- It does not sell goods, run events, take payments, or broker transactions.
- It does not operate a jobs board.
- It does not verify the identity of individual users.
- It does not provide merchant analytics or reach reporting.
