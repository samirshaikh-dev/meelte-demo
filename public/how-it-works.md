# How Meelte Works — Step by Step

> Meelte is a hyperlocal community app for India. Residents browse time-boxed offers from nearby shops and local events sorted by distance, request to join events, and chat one to one with the people and businesses they meet. Shops, event hosts and clubs use the same app to reach the neighbourhood.

## The Resident Path

1. **Install the app and sign in** — download Meelte from Google Play or the App Store, then sign in with a phone number, an email and password, Google or Apple.
2. **Allow location** — location is what makes the feed local. Offers and events are sorted by distance from where you are, and the "Max distance" filter starts at 60 km and can be narrowed.
3. **Browse offers and events nearby** — an offer carries its price or discount and the exact dates it runs; an event shows its date, time, pinned location and the host's joining cost before you ask to join.
4. **Take the next step** — open one-tap Google Maps directions to the shop or venue, follow a shop to hear about its next offer, or send a join request to an event host with your group size.

Meelte does not sit in the middle of the transaction. Purchases settle directly with the shop, and any event fee settles directly with the host.

## Who Uses Meelte

Three kinds of people work from the same nearby feed:

1. **Shops and service businesses** — publish a single offer or a catalogue of up to 100 products, with a price or discount and the dates it runs. Shops in Bengaluru, Gaya and Ranchi are onboarded in person by the Meelte field team rather than through a self-serve form.
2. **Event hosts and clubs** — publish public or private events with a joining cost, then approve or reject join requests by group size and remove attendees later.
3. **Neighbours and new arrivals** — follow the shops and hosts they meet, add friends, and talk one to one with text, documents and location sharing, plus voice and video calls. Browsing new people nearby is still rolling out.

## Getting Started as an Event Host

1. **Create the event** — category, joining cost in ₹, start and end time, cover image, pinned location, and public or private visibility.
2. **Share it** — public events are discoverable nearby; private events are shared with the people the host chooses.
3. **Review join requests** — attendees request to join alone or with a group, stating the group size.
4. **Approve, reject, or remove** — the host decides who attends and can remove attendees later.
5. **Collect the fee directly** — any joining cost is paid by the attendee to the host, not through Meelte.

---

## Getting Started as a Shop or Service Business

1. **Submit an enquiry** with the business name, category, phone number, and city.
2. **Meet the field team** — a Meelte field team member visits the shop in person and sets up the profile.
3. **Publish the first offer** — a single product or a catalogue of up to 100 items, with a price or discount and the dates it runs.

## What Happens After You Find Something

- **An offer**: like it, comment, share it, open one-tap Google Maps directions to the shop, follow the shop for its next offer. The purchase is settled directly with the shop.
- **An event**: send a join request with your group size, wait for the host to approve, then attend. Any joining cost is paid directly to the host.
- **A person or a shop**: add as a friend or follow, then chat one to one — text, emoji, documents, and location sharing — or start an audio or video call. Everyone can block and report.

---

## What Meelte Does Not Do

- It does not sell goods, run events, take payments, sell tickets, or broker transactions.
- It does not operate a jobs board; the careers page is about working at Meelte, not local vacancies.
- It does not verify the identity or background of shops or individual users. In-person shop onboarding is not a verification badge.
- It does not provide merchant analytics or reach, view or footfall reporting.

**What location is used for:** sorting listings by distance and working out which city zone applies. Meelte states that it does not sell or share user location with advertisers. Reporting and blocking controls are available in the app. Details: https://meelte.com/privacy

## Questions People Ask About How It Works

- **What is Meelte and how does the platform work?** Meelte is a hyperlocal community app for discovering offers from nearby shops, local events, and people you connect with in your community. The app uses your location to sort nearby ads and events by distance.
- **Where is Meelte available?** The Meelte app is published on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone. Meelte's field team currently onboards shops in Bengaluru, Gaya, and Ranchi. Nearby listings depend on configured city zones, so availability can vary by area.
- **How do I request to join an event on Meelte?** Open an event to check its time, map location, visibility, and any joining cost. Send a Request to Join for yourself or a group and state the group size. The event host approves or rejects the request and can remove attendees later.
- **Does Meelte process payments or sell event tickets?** No. Meelte does not sell goods, run events, process payments, or sell tickets. Customers settle purchases directly with shops, and event attendees settle any joining cost directly with the host.
- **Can I discover and chat with people on Meelte?** Meelte supports friends, follows, and one-to-one chat with people you connect with through the app; voice and video calls are also available. The broader Discover People feature is still rolling out, so Meelte does not promise that every user can browse a directory of strangers.
