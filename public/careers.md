## Current Openings

**No open roles at the moment.** Meelte is not actively hiring right now. If you believe you would be a great fit, a speculative application is still welcome.

How to apply:

1. Email **hr@meelte.com** with the role you are interested in as the subject.
2. Include your resume and links to your LinkedIn, GitHub, or portfolio.
3. Meelte replies if there is a good fit.

## What Meelte Values

- **Community-first** — build for neighbourhoods, putting real people and local communities at the heart of the work.
- **Build for Bharat** — focus on India's towns and cities, solving everyday problems with simple, useful experiences.
- **Ownership** — take initiative, move with purpose, and own outcomes in a small, tight-knit team.
- **Empathy** — listen first, stay humble, and design with care for the people who use Meelte every day.

---

## Working at Meelte

- **Flexible work** — outcomes over hours.
- **Learning and growth** — learning resources, mentorship, and opportunities to grow across the stack.
- **Mission-driven impact** — work that helps real people in neighbourhoods across India.
- **Small, collaborative team** — close work with founders, where ideas have immediate impact.
- **Health and wellbeing** — support for a healthy work-life balance.
- **Equity and ownership** — early team members can share in Meelte's growth.
